# Plooy – Audio Licensing & Attribution
Patir Studios (2025)

This project uses a combination of **original audio**, **CC0/Public Domain assets**,  
**CC-BY assets requiring attribution**, and **YouTube-licensed sound effects** that  
permit reuse in creative projects. All audio used complies with Game Jam rules  
and is legally included under the terms listed below.

No copyrighted, paid, or restricted audio was used.

---

# 1. Original Audio (Created by Jakim D. Lopez – Sound Designer)
The following categories of audio were created, recorded, or composed specifically  
for Plooy and are copyrighted by Patir Studios (2025):

- Character voice reactions / vocal SFX across levels  
- Minor UI sound variations 
- **Music:**  
  - Plooy Main Theme  
  - Level 11 Theme  

These original assets may NOT be reused outside this game without permission.

---

# 2. CC0 / Public Domain Audio
The following categories include CC0-licensed assets from open libraries  
such as opengameart.org Pixabay, Freesound CC0 pool, BFXR, and similar sources.

These sounds require **no attribution** and are free for any use:

- Environmental ambience (forest, wind, graveyard, idle wind, etc.)  
- Mechanical interaction SFX (clanks, impacts, metal hits, etc.)  
- Creature / animal SFX (dinosaur growls, bird chirps, etc.)  
- Miscellaneous game SFX (glass break, rock impacts, etc.)  
- Generated noise from BFXR (UI bleeps, simple tones)

All CC0 audio is covered under Public Domain and imposes no restrictions.

---

Some sound effects originate from creators who published their work

# 4. YouTube-Sourced Audio
Some SFX were sourced from YouTube creators who explicitly allow reuse  
and modification of their audio under Creative Commons or Free-to-Use terms.  

All YouTube assets were taken only from videos explicitly labeled as:
- **CC0**,  
- **CC-BY**,  
- **Free to Use**,  
- **Free SFX**,  
- or **No Copyright SFX**.

Due to Game Jam time constraints, exact video links may not all be listed,  
but all selected audio came from legal reuse-friendly uploads.
