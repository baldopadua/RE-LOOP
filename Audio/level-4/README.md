# Level 4 Audio Assets - Dinosaur Evolution Theme

## Planned Audio Files:

### Incubator Sounds:
- incubator_processing.wav - When materials are being processed
- incubator_complete.wav - When T-Rex egg is ready
- incubator_error.wav - When wrong materials are used

### Evolution Sounds:
- egg_crack.wav - T-Rex hatching sound
- trex_roar.wav - T-Rex appearance/growth sound
- bone_drop.wav - Bone hitting incubator
- stick_drop.wav - Stick hitting incubator

### Creature Sounds:
- chicken_cluck.wav - Chicken interactions
- dog_bark.wav - Dog interactions
- lizard_hiss.wav - Lizard interactions

### Environment:
- prehistoric_ambience.wav - Background atmosphere

## Audio Integration:
These sounds will be integrated following Klarenz's sound_manager.gd patterns.
